About

I'm the engineer at a five-person AI consultancy in the Netherlands, which in practice means I build the thing, deploy it, write the documentation, sit in the client meeting, and then explain it to the board.

In four months I've put an assistant live inside a client's own Azure tenant twenty days after the first commit, rebuilt an insurer's risk-assessment process as a tool where the AI proposes and a human decides, and built our entire back office - invoicing, e-signing, bank reconciliation, VAT returns - because buying five subscriptions was the worse option.

Before that: an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam, and three published papers on text simplification and fairness in graph neural networks.

I build with agentic coding tools day to day, and I say so to clients rather than letting them find out later.
