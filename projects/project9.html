Internal Back Office Platform

Project Overview

Everything arriving through a company website — enquiries, training leads, job applications — handled all the way through to a signed quote, a sent invoice, a matched bank payment, and monthly search-visibility numbers.

I built it end to end as the sole engineer: design, code, infrastructure, tests, deploy.

Key Features

  • Invoicing with an in-house PDF generator
  • Its own e-signing flow — drawn signature, timestamp, IP, expiry, evidence retention
  • Bank reconciliation over an open-banking API
  • Expense capture with AI categorisation feeding VAT-return tables
  • A Word-like article editor that publishes straight to the public site's repo
  • Cookieless analytics that store no IPs and set no cookies

Technical Implementation

It also runs a monthly measurement asking the same questions across ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, scoring whether the company shows up at all. The first baseline: 0 of 51 answers mentioned it.

Node.js, Postgres, vanilla JS, hand-built SVG charts, pdfkit, Playwright, Railway, Cloudflare.

Results

141 commits over 19 working days · 22 database tables · 11 Playwright suites · replaced three paid subscriptions.
